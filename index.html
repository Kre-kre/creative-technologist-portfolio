(03) AI

A NEW KIND OF TOOLKIT.

AI has changed how quickly an idea can move.

I use it to research, explore, prototype, write, build and iterate — collapsing the distance between disciplines and making more room for judgment, curiosity and experimentation.

Research→ Explore→ Prototype→ Write→ Build→ Iterate ↺
Technology is the tool. The engine is human.
(04) ABOUT

I’m Gargi — a finance & economics graduate, writer and designer interested in the space between business, technology and creativity.

I’ve always been drawn to ideas looking for direction, products looking for shape, stories looking for a world.

So I tend to work across disciplines rather than inside one. Strategy. Design. Technology. Writing. Whatever the idea calls for.

Currently building, experimenting and figuring out what becomes possible when the distance between imagining and making gets smaller.
